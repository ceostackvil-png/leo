Product Description Manufacture, Care and Fit

Break free from the weekday routine with Nobero’s Colourblock Utility Co-ord Sets — a bold blend of comfort, street-style, and versatility. Crafted from a breathable premium cotton blend, this co-ord is made for adventure, travel, lounging, and everything in between.

The oversized pullover features a crew neck with modern cut-and-sew colorblock panels for a trendy, athletic edge. Paired with straight-fit bottoms that offer zippered closure, welt pockets, and a relaxed silhouette — this set delivers function and flair in equal measure.

Key Features:
• Premium Cotton Blend — Breathable & Stretch-Ready
• Oversized Crewneck Pullover with Cut-and-Sew Colorblock
• Straight-Fit Bottoms with Zipper Fly and Utility Pockets
• Ideal for Travel, Weekend Outings & Loungewear Looks
• Made in India

Care:
Machine wash cold with similar colors. Tumble dry low or hang dry. Do not bleach. Iron inside out.
